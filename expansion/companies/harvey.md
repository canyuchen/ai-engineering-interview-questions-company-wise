- **q-b4777ef85ec6b8** `community_question_bank` — Is the clause in the parsed document at all?** Pull the extracted text and grep it. If the clause is missing, this is an ingestion bug: a scanned page that OCR mangled, a page dropped by the PDF parser, text trapped in a table or a footnote, or the clause living in an amendment that was never attached to the base agreement. In practice this is the single most common cause and the cheapest to check. [Source / reading](https://github.com/ombharatiya/AI-Engineer-Interview-Questions/blob/108530e6bf6b118b23599668d8e1c9fdae054fed/14-company-interview-questions/harvey.md#L389)
- **q-b90f31e703e2b4** `community_question_bank` — Was the chunk retrieved?** Replay the query and inspect the top-k. If the text is present but not retrieved, the cause is chunking (the clause split across two chunks, so neither is a strong match), vocabulary (the contract says &quot;Assignment upon Change in Control&quot; while the query says &quot;change of control&quot;), or reranker behaviour. Hybrid retrieval and clause-boundary chunking are the fixes, per Q1. [Source / reading](https://github.com/ombharatiya/AI-Engineer-Interview-Questions/blob/108530e6bf6b118b23599668d8e1c9fdae054fed/14-company-interview-questions/harvey.md#L390)
