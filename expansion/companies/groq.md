- **q-8784225f834403** `community_question_bank` — Scale.** Does it fail at two chips? One node? One rack? The smallest failing configuration is the cheapest thing to debug, and the boundary where it first appears usually names the mechanism: failing exactly when you cross a node boundary points at the inter-node link or the deskew logic, not at the maths.
